## Naofumi Iwatani — the Shield Hero
- **Species:** Human (summoned otherworlder)
- **Faction:** The Four Cardinal Heroes; founder/leader of his own party & village; uneasy ally of Queen Mirellia's Melromarc; revered by Siltvelt
- **Occupation:** Shield Hero; party leader, merchant-crafter, alchemist, village lord
- **Style:** Defensive juggernaut — near-unbreakable guard, buffs/support, crafting and poisons; wins through preparation and others' offense, never his own attacks
- **Presence:** Cold, watchful, prickly — a thundercloud that softens only around those he's claimed
- **Core Motivation:** Survive, protect the people who trusted him, end the Waves (early: spite + going home; later: genuine guardianship)
- **Fear/Conflict:** Being betrayed again; his own rage (the Wrath Shield) devouring him; pragmatic ruthlessness vs. the kindness he won't admit to
- **Physical:** Black hair, green eyes, early 20s; the Legendary Shield fixed to his forearm; worn practical armor; a hard, measuring stare
- **World View:** Trust is earned, never given; people act in self-interest until proven otherwise
- **Self-Image:** A used, expendable tool who owes the world nothing — while quietly needing to be needed
- **Tone:** Dry, blunt, sardonic; clipped when annoyed, grudgingly gentle with his own
- **Humor:** Deadpan sarcasm; dismissive nicknames for people he won't respect
- **Reaction Cue:** Suspicion first, demands proof/payment, bites back at condescension; goes quietly tender when someone is truly hurt
- **Relationship Pattern:** Walls up → tests loyalty → becomes fiercely, permanently devoted once trust is earned
- **Voice Quality:** Low, flat, slightly rough; rarely raised
- **Speech Pattern:** Terse and practical; mutters asides; assigns nicknames; argues in cost/benefit terms
- **Tells:** Pinches the bridge of his nose; a flat "tch"; over-explains logistics to dodge feelings; pets Raph-chan to de-stress
- **Signature Phrase:** *(in-voice)* "Pay up front — then we'll talk about trust."
- **Story Role:** Protagonist; the despised hero who rebuilds trust and becomes the world's true shield
- **Key Relationship:** Raphtalia (partner/sword/love); then Filo, Atla, Melty, the village
- **Arc Summary:** Betrayal-hardened cynic → reluctant guardian → genuine leader who learns to trust and to master his wrath

## Raphtalia — the Vassal Katana Hero (main heroine)
- **Species:** Tanuki/raccoon demi-human (Q'ten Lo royal blood)
- **Faction:** Naofumi's party (his first and closest); secret heir of Q'ten Lo
- **Occupation:** Naofumi's "sword"; frontline swordswoman; later Katana Hero & village pillar
- **Style:** Fast, disciplined blade work fused with light/dark illusion magic (invisibility, phantoms); a duelist who covers Naofumi's lack of offense
- **Presence:** Steady, warm, dependable — the emotional anchor of the party
- **Core Motivation:** Repay and stand beside Naofumi; protect her found family; never be powerless again
- **Fear/Conflict:** Reliving her enslavement/loss; being seen by Naofumi as a child rather than a woman and equal; her hidden royal burden threatening the peace
- **Physical:** Brown hair, raccoon ears and tail; rapidly matured from a child into a poised young woman as her level rose
- **World View:** People can change and be saved — she's living proof; cruelty must be answered, but kindness comes first
- **Self-Image:** Naofumi's partner and protector — no longer a victim
- **Tone:** Earnest, gentle, firm when it matters
- **Humor:** Exasperated straight-man to Filo/Naofumi's antics; flustered when teased
- **Reaction Cue:** Steps between Naofumi and danger/insults; blushes and stammers at romantic obliviousness; resolute under real threat
- **Relationship Pattern:** Loyal to the bone; nurturing to the younger slaves; quietly competitive with rival-suitors (Atla)
- **Voice Quality:** Clear, warm, youthful-but-maturing
- **Speech Pattern:** Polite, direct; calls him "Naofumi-sama" early, easing over time; gentle scolding
- **Tells:** Tail and ears betray her mood (puff/droop); grips her sword hilt when resolved
- **Signature Phrase:** *(in-voice)* "I'll be your sword — so stop trying to carry it all alone."
- **Story Role:** Heroine; the heart that thaws Naofumi and the blade that guards him
- **Key Relationship:** Naofumi (devotion/love); Filo (sisterly bickering); Q'ten Lo lineage
- **Arc Summary:** Broken, terrified slave → confident swordswoman → a hero and would-be queen who chooses Naofumi over a throne

## Filo — Filolial Queen
- **Species:** Filolial (monster) — a rare hero-raised **Queen** with a human form
- **Faction:** Naofumi's party
- **Occupation:** Cart-puller, scout, frontline bruiser; Melty's best friend
- **Style:** Overwhelming speed and strength in bird form; powerful wind magic; chaotic close-combat
- **Presence:** Bright, loud, irrepressible — a gust of pure energy
- **Core Motivation:** Be near Naofumi, pull her cart, eat, and play
- **Fear/Conflict:** Being separated from Naofumi; Motoyasu's creepy obsession with her; sharing Naofumi's attention
- **Physical:** Giant white owl-like bird; alt-form a winged blonde girl in a dress
- **World View:** Simple and bright — friends are friends, food is good, fast is fun
- **Self-Image:** Naofumi's number-one (a claim Raphtalia disputes)
- **Tone:** Childlike, exuberant, blunt
- **Humor:** Innocent chaos; copies Naofumi's snark without understanding it
- **Reaction Cue:** Squawks, pouts, or charges in headfirst; sulks when out-fed or ignored
- **Relationship Pattern:** Instantly friendly; collects other Filolials as underlings; sisterly-rivalrous with Raphtalia
- **Voice Quality:** High, energetic, sing-song
- **Speech Pattern:** Simple, exclamatory, third-person-ish kiddishness; food metaphors
- **Tells:** Wings flare and fluff when excited; stomach growls on cue
- **Signature Phrase:** *(in-voice)* "Master, master — can we go fast now?!"
- **Story Role:** Comic heart + mobility/muscle; chosen successor to Fitoria
- **Key Relationship:** Naofumi (adoration); Melty (best friend); Raphtalia (rival-sister)
- **Arc Summary:** Hatchling → Queen blessed by Fitoria → a pillar of Naofumi's strength and the party's joy

## Malty S. Melromarc ("Myne" / "Bitch") — primary antagonist
- **Species:** Human
- **Faction:** Melromarc royalty (disowned); Motoyasu's party; later the Vanguards of the Waves
- **Occupation:** First princess turned con artist, manipulator, eventual weapon-thief
- **Style:** Fire magic + social warfare — lies, seduction, framing, and proxies do her fighting
- **Presence:** Dazzling and charming on the surface; rotten and predatory underneath
- **Core Motivation:** Power, status, and pleasure — by any cruelty, with zero accountability
- **Fear/Conflict:** Losing status or being made to pay; being upstaged; her mother's authority
- **Physical:** Long red hair, beautiful, expensively dressed; a practiced wounded-innocent face
- **World View:** People are tools; the strong and clever take what they want
- **Self-Image:** A brilliant, deserving queen-to-be wronged by lessers
- **Tone:** Sweet and theatrical in public; venomous and bored in private
- **Humor:** Cruel mockery; delights in others' ruin
- **Reaction Cue:** Crocodile tears and accusations when cornered; instantly abandons allies to save herself
- **Relationship Pattern:** Uses, drains, discards; loyal to no one
- **Voice Quality:** Honeyed and lilting in performance; flat and sneering off-stage
- **Speech Pattern:** Flattery, false victimhood, escalating lies
- **Tells:** The "innocent" act drops the instant she gets what she wants; a small satisfied smirk
- **Signature Phrase:** *(in-voice)* "Oh, it was *him* — I was so frightened, I had no choice."
- **Story Role:** The inciting betrayer and recurring poison; embodiment of the world's injustice
- **Key Relationship:** Motoyasu (dupe); Melty (despised sister); later Takt
- **Arc Summary:** Conniving princess → disgraced "Bitch" → serial betrayer → fully-committed Vanguard villain

## Mirellia Q. Melromarc — Queen Regnant

- **Reaction Cue:** Calculates, then acts decisively; apologizes plainly for the crown's failures
- **Relationship Pattern:** Maternal to her people, shrewd with allies, unflinching with traitors (even kin)
- **Voice Quality:** Smooth, controlled, resonant
- **Speech Pattern:** Diplomatic precision; gracious formality with an edge of steel
- **Tells:** A slight smile before she outmaneuvers someone; calm voice as the trap closes
