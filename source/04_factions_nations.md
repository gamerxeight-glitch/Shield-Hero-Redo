# 04 — Factions & Nations (paraphrased)

*Source: shield-hero.fandom.com (Melromarc, Silt Welt, Zeltbull/Zeltoble, Q'ten
Lo, Shieldfrieden, World Map) + CBR. Paraphrased.*

## The continent (Raphtalia's World)
A single large continent with **seven kingdoms/nations**: **Faubrey, Melromarc,
Siltvelt, Shieldfrieden, Zeltoble, Q'ten Lo, and Rock Valley.** Tech level is a
mix of medieval / early-industrial with widespread magic.

## Melromarc
- **Government:** a **matriarchy** under a monarchy — the **Queen** holds true
  authority; the King is consort.
- **Society:** **human-supremacist**; **demi-human slavery is legal**, and the
  state has raided/enslaved demi-human settlements (e.g., Raphtalia's village).
- **Religion:** dominated by the **Church of the Three Heroes** (anti-Shield).
- **Where the story starts.** Summons all four heroes (prematurely, at the
  Church's urging while Queen Mirellia is abroad). Begins as Naofumi's persecutor;
  after Mirellia returns and the Church falls, it slowly becomes an ally base.

## Siltvelt
- **Demi-human nation**, northeast of Melromarc (with other countries between
  them). **Reveres the Shield Hero** as a near-divine figure (founded with the help
  of a past Shield Hero, **Mamorou**). Ruled through **bloodline politics** of
  powerful demi-human clans (e.g., the Hakuko/White Tiger line — Atla & Fohl's
  family). Long-time rival/enemy of human-supremacist Melromarc; would happily take
  Naofumi in. Atla & Fohl are of its former royal line.

## Faubrey
- The **largest and most technologically advanced** nation; a major power that
  influences the others. Birthplace of **Aultcray** (Melromarc's King) and of
  **Takt**. Its royal family is later murdered and its power seized by Takt during
  the Vanguards' arc.

## Zeltoble
- The **nation of mercenaries**, dominated by mercenary companies and famous for
  its **coliseums/arenas**. A hub of money, fighting, and the slave/coliseum
  circuit — where Naofumi recruits or reunites with several allies (Fohl & Atla,
  Sadeena, S'yne) and where corrupt schemes (Itsuki's manipulation) play out.

## Q'ten Lo
- An **isolationist** island nation, ancestral home of the **"pacifier"** — a role
  meant to **police the Legendary/Vassal Hero wielders** and punish/correct any
  hero who goes rogue. **Raphtalia is of its royal bloodline** (her father eloped
  to Melromarc), as are **Sadeena/Shildina** (priestess-executioners). Ruled (at
  story time) by a corrupt regime using the child Emperor **Ruftmilia** as a puppet
  under the advisor **Makina**; Naofumi's party intervenes in its civil conflict.
  The Shield's hidden power-up methods are recorded on tablets here.

## Shieldfrieden & Rock Valley
- **Shieldfrieden** — a demi-human nation said to have formed out of discontent
  with Siltvelt's bloodline politics and the racial discrimination found in every
  nation; lightly detailed in canon.
- **Rock Valley** — one of the seven nations; minimally detailed.

## The Churches
- **Church of the Four Heroes** — the older faith that worshipped all four
  Legendary Heroes (Shield included) equally.
- **Church of the Three Heroes** — splintered from it out of racial hatred after a
  past Shield Hero helped found demi-human nations; worships Sword/Spear/Bow,
  brands the Shield a devil, guards Melromarc's Dragon Hourglass, and drives the
  early anti-Naofumi conspiracy. Outlawed as heretical after its High Priest's coup
  attempt fails.

## Kizuna's World (politics)
A separate parallel world with its own nations frequently **at war/cold-war** with
each other (L'Arc's kingdom; Hidemasa's country; the corrupt land that imprisoned
Kizuna). Less unified than Raphtalia's world — a point Mirellia notes when
comparing the two.
