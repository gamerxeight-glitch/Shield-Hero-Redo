# 01 — Characters (paraphrased)

Grouped by role. Personality + arc notes condensed in our own words.
*Source: Wikipedia character list + shield-hero.fandom.com. Paraphrased.*

## The Four Legendary Heroes (Naofumi's world)

### Naofumi Iwatani — the Shield Hero (protagonist)
University student summoned after opening a book about the Four Heroes. Starts
open-minded; turns cynical and guarded after being scorned by the other heroes,
shunned for religious prejudice, robbed, and falsely accused of assault by Malty.
Begins to soften only once Raphtalia stands by him. Pragmatic to the point of
ruthless about his party's survival, will use methods the other heroes find
distasteful (paying upfront, slave-crest "honesty"), but fiercely protective of
those who treat him as an equal. Can defend but not directly attack; immense
defensive power and support/crafting ability. Later wields the **Wrath Shield**
(curse series) when consumed by rage.

### Motoyasu Kitamura — the Spear Hero
Killed in his own world, summoned in. Kind but extremely gullible and
womanizing; led by an all-female "cheer squad" party and easily manipulated by
Malty, whom he believes over Naofumi for a long time. Physically the strongest
hero. After Malty's betrayal he unlocks the **Spears of Lust/Envy** (curse),
which leave him seeing/hearing most women as pig-like — his sanity frays and he
fixates obsessively on Filo. (Protagonist of the spin-off *The Reprise of the
Spear Hero*, where dying grants him time-travel back to the start.)

### Ren Amaki — the Sword Hero
Youngest hero; a loner who treats the world like a single-player RPG and runs his
party like a guild of individuals. Condescending toward lower-levels. After his
party is wiped out by the Spirit Tortoise he spirals, is robbed by Malty, becomes
a bandit, and unlocks the **Swords of Greed/Gluttony** (curse). Eventually owns
his mistakes and becomes Eclair's student (and later trains toward smithing).

### Itsuki Kawasumi — the Bow Hero
Self-styled "ally of justice" with a superiority complex and a need for praise;
runs his party as a vigilante hierarchy. Frames/ejects Rishia out of jealousy.
Betrayed and left for dead by his party at the Spirit Tortoise; manipulated by
Malty into fighting in Zeltoble, unlocking the **Bow of Pride** (curse). Broken
and humbled afterward; recovers in Naofumi's village. Later wields the Vassal
Musical Instrument in Kizuna's world.

## Naofumi's party / Lurolona (Raphtalia's) Village

### Raphtalia — main heroine; later the Vassal Katana Hero
Tanuki/raccoon demi-human orphaned by the first Wave, enslaved, and tortured by a
cruel noble before Naofumi buys her as his "sword." Under his protection she heals
and becomes a superb swordswoman; sees through his bitterness and falls for him.
Demi-humans' bodies mature with levels, so she rapidly grows from a child into a
young woman (and bristles when Naofumi treats her as a daughter). Specializes in
light/dark magic (illusions, invisibility). Secretly heir to **Q'ten Lo**'s
throne, giving her a bloodline ability to "pacify" hero weapons. In Kizuna's world
she's chosen by the **Vassal Katana**.

### Filo — Filolial Queen
Hatched by Naofumi from an egg. Filolials are cart-pulling bird-monsters; raised
by a hero, Filo becomes a rare **Queen** who can shapeshift into a winged blonde
girl. Boundless energy, huge appetite, short attention span; competes with
Raphtalia/Gaelion for Naofumi's attention. Strong wind magic; favorite hobby is
pulling her custom cart. Blessed with power by the previous Queen, **Fitoria**.

### Raph-chan
A shikigami/familiar Naofumi creates from Raphtalia's hair + his blood to track
the real Raphtalia in Kizuna's world. Resembles Raphtalia, uses light/dark magic,
can always find her "parents." Naofumi dotes on her obsessively; the village's
tamed monsters imitate her, spawning the comedic "Raph species." (Raphtalia finds
the whole thing mortifying.)

### Keel
Raphtalia's childhood friend, a dog demi-human; tomboyish, initially assumed
herself male. Becomes an "older-brother"-style figure's tag-along ("Bubba
Shield"), leader of the village's younger ex-slaves, and helps Naofumi's trade
business. Gains a puppy-like therianthrope form.

### Atla & Fohl — White Tiger (Hakuko) demi-human siblings
Grandchildren of a former Siltvelt king. Fohl sold himself into Zeltoble's
coliseums to buy medicine for the sickly, blind **Atla**; Naofumi buys both and
cures Atla. **Atla**: blind, serenely ruthless on Naofumi's behalf, a martial-arts
prodigy who senses chi; deeply in love with Naofumi; dies shielding him at the
Phoenix battle, unlocking his **Blessed Shield of Compassion**, and lingers as a
guiding spirit. **Fohl**: proud martial artist, overprotective of Atla, later
becomes the **Seven Star Gauntlet Hero**.

### Sadeena & Shildina — Orca demi-human sisters (from Q'ten Lo)
**Sadeena**: motherly, hard-drinking, flirts with Naofumi; a former royal
executioner/priestess loyal to Raphtalia's father; extraordinarily strong (can
break her own slave curse); later the **Vassal Harpoon Hero**. **Shildina**:
Sadeena's scatterbrained younger sister, also a priestess/executioner who can
channel past warriors; resentful of being treated as Sadeena's "replacement";
later the **Vassal Ofuda Hero**.

### Others in the village
- **Rishia Ivyred** — timid ex-noble cast off by Itsuki; Naofumi trains her; a
  hidden-talent fighter who shines in crises; learns Hengen Muso under Elrasla;
  becomes the **Seven Star Projectile Hero**.
- **Imiya** — shy Lumo (mole) demi-human; gifted accessory-crafter.
- **Wyndia** — dog demi-human, monster-lover; cares for the village's monsters
  and the dragon **Gaelion**.
- **Ratotille (Rat) Anthreya** — monster researcher/alchemist exiled from Faubrey.
- **Gaelion** — a dragon Naofumi raises from an egg (secretly also carries the
  consciousness of a dead Dragon Emperor).
- **Ruftmilia** — Raphtalia's cousin; former child Emperor of Q'ten Lo used as a
  puppet ruler.

## Melromarc royalty & state

- **Mirellia Q. Melromarc** — the true ruler (Queen Regnant); shrewd diplomat,
  ice magic, commands ninja-like "Shadows." Unprejudiced toward demi-humans;
  returns to crush the Church, punish her husband/daughter, and back Naofumi.
  Later killed in an ambush set by Takt & Malty.
- **Melty Q. Melromarc** — second princess and heir; kind, water magic, skilled
  diplomat, Filo's best friend. Becomes Queen after Mirellia's death.
- **Aultcray Melromarc XXXII ("Trash")** — King Consort and **Seven Star Staff
  Hero**; born a Faubrey prince (real name Lüge Lansarz Faubrey). Fiercely
  anti-demi-human due to family tragedies; enabler of Naofumi's early persecution.
  Stripped of rank (legally renamed "Trash"); slowly, partially redeemed.
- **Eclair Seaetto** — principled noblewoman/knight; cares more for training than
  governing; respects Naofumi as a fighter though they clash on morals.
- **Erhard** — Castletown blacksmith; the *first* person to believe in Naofumi.
- **Beloukas ("the Slave Trader")** — cunning black-market slave/monster merchant
  Naofumi repeatedly does business with.
- **Elrasla Ragnarok ("the Hengen Muso Lady")** — aged martial-arts master, last
  practitioner of the Life-Force style; trains the village (esp. Rishia).

## Kizuna's World (parallel) — heroes & allies

- **Kizuna Kazayama — the Hunting Hero** — summoned earlier than the others to
  fight a Demon Dragon; trapped years in a labyrinth until Naofumi frees her.
  Cheerful, otaku, obsessive fisher; can only damage monsters (like Naofumi can
  only defend). Glass's best friend; inspires Naofumi to found his village.
- **Glass — Vassal Fan Hero** — stern, honorable "Spirit Person" whose stats are
  tied to Soul Power; initially attacks Naofumi's world to save her own, later a
  staunch ally. From a fallen kingdom with "pacifier"-like blood (needs Shildina's
  help to use it).
- **L'Arc Berg Sickle — Vassal Scythe Hero** — easygoing young king; befriends
  Naofumi ("kiddo") before realizing he's the Shield Hero; later allies with him.
- **Therese Alexanderite** — L'Arc's companion, a "Jewel" who channels gemstone
  magic; fascinated by Naofumi's crafting.
- **Ethnobalt — Vassal Book Hero** — leader of the scholarly Library Rabbits;
  shapeshifts into a boy; support specialist.
- **S'yne Lokk — Vassal Sewing-Set Hero** — quiet, near-emotionless sole survivor
  of a destroyed world; fights with scissors/threads/familiars; her translation
  ability is broken (speech comes out static).

## Antagonists

### Malty S. Melromarc ("Myne" / legally "Bitch") — primary antagonist
Disowned first princess. Frames Naofumi on day one; manipulates Motoyasu; sadistic,
power-hungry, fire magic. Disgraced and slave-cursed by her mother, she keeps
betraying everyone (Motoyasu, Ren, Itsuki), joins Takt, and ultimately becomes a
**Vanguard of the Waves**.

### The Church of the Three Heroes
Melromarc's dominant religion; worships Sword/Spear/Bow and brands the Shield a
demon. Splintered from the older **Church of the Four Heroes** out of racial
hatred after a past Shield Hero helped found demi-human nations. Engineers the
early summoning and Naofumi's persecution; its High Priest (anime: **Biscas T.
Balmus**) tries to kill all four heroes and seize the throne before Naofumi stops
him; Mirellia then outlaws the Church.

### Vanguards of the Waves
The series' overarching human conspiracy: people reincarnated into other worlds by
"God" (Medea), chosen for their selfish traits, given powers to steal hero weapons
and resurrect. Key members:
- **Kyo Ethnina** — rogue Vassal Book Hero/scientist from Kizuna's world; awakens
  and hijacks the **Spirit Tortoise** to harvest souls; the season-2 villain.
- **Takt Alsaholn Faubrey** — Faubrey noble, former Seven Star Whip Hero; builds a
  brainwashed harem, steals multiple weapons, murders the Faubrey royals and
  Mirellia, declares war on Melromarc.
- **S'yne's older sister — Vassal Chain Hero** — cold, toying high-ranking
  Vanguard tied to the destruction of S'yne's home world.
- **Hidemasa Miyaji** — envious extra-summoned hero turned Vanguard (Vassal
  Musical Instrument).
- **Mald ("Armor")** — ex-Itsuki party member; later wields a stolen Seven Star Axe.

### Other notable
- **Fitoria** — centuries-old Filolial Queen and lost **Vassal Carriage Hero**;
  guardian of the world who will force the hero cycle (even kill heroes) to keep
  the Waves contained; mentors Filo.
- **Ost Horai** — humanlike familiar/manifestation of the Spirit Tortoise; helps
  Naofumi destroy it at the cost of her life; later a guiding spirit.
- **Demon Dragon Emperor** — parallel-world dragon whose core powers Naofumi's
  shield; manifests as the **Wrath Dragon** embodying Naofumi's rage; later
  revived as a (female) ally bound loosely to Naofumi.
- **Mamorou Shirono** — the legendary past Shield Hero and founder of Siltvelt
  (encountered via time-travel in later LN arcs); created Fitoria and engineered
  Siltvelt's ruling demi-human races.
- **Yomogi Emarl** — Kyo's well-meaning childhood friend who defects to the heroes.
