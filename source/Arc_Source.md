** Arc 1: Summoning **
Naofumi Iwatani is a carefree college sophomore and a so-called otaku. One day, he goes into the library and searches for a fantasy novel and finds The Record of the Four Holy Weapons. Suddenly Naofumi's vision blurs and he falls unconscious.
Naofumi wakes up in a castle along with three other "heroes" with weapons. To his surprise, he also has a shield attached to his arm. The four are introduced to the reigning King and the three other heroes casually introduce themselves. Ren Amaki as the Sword Hero, Motoyasu Kitamura as the Spear Hero and Itsuki Kawasumi as the Bow Hero. The four heroes are then introduced to the land and told that they were summoned to defeat the dangerous "waves" plaguing the land. Later, the four heroes meet in their designated quarters and realize that they all have been summoned from different worlds. While Naofumi is confused about the scenario, the others take it in stride since they believe that they are in a game which they have played before.
The next day, the heroes are assigned companions. At first, no one wants to work with Naofumi however an adventurer named Myne Suphia decides to join him. The heroes then start their own training and Naofumi realizes that he is far behind the other heroes since he cannot harm enemies like the others. He also goes shopping at the weapon store and Myne gets him nice armor.
The next day, Naofumi wakes up with all of his possessions missing. Soldiers then come and drag him to the castle where Myne is crying and frames him for raping her. Naofumi is stunned while the heroes (believing the charges) are about to kill him. It is revealed that Myne is actually named Malty S Melromarc and is the king's daughter. In the middle of the night she had stolen all of his valuables and then ran of the other heroes with false claims that Naofumi had raped her. Naofumi is then exiled from the castle with no possessions. With the rumours spreading quickly, he is outcast from society. 

** Arc 2: Slave **
Naofumi's reputation is in tatters. After Malty spread the false claims of him raping her, everyone refused to do business with him. Erhard, the weapon shop owner nearly pummelled Naofumi until he saw the truth in Naofumi's face. In pity, the shop owner gives Naofumi some basic clothes.
Naofumi attempted to level up by himself. However, due to the limitations of the shield, he unable to damage monsters. Instead, Naofumi learns to collect ingredients and sells it to the apothecary who is apathetic to Naofumi's reputation. Naofumi is eventually approached by a strange man who introduces him to the prospect of buying a slave. While Naofumi cannot attack monsters, his slave can do it for him. Also, in this kingdom of Melromarc, a race called demi-humans are discriminated against and are commonly used as slaves. Seeing no other option, Naofumi decides to buy the cheapest slave - a deathly sick and mentally ill demi-human. Naofumi then learned that her name is Raphtalia.
Now having a slave, Naofumi takes her to the weapons shop to get equipment and then to a tavern for food. He gives her a proper kids meal while he gets the cheapest food since he cannot taste anything since the deception. The two then start hunting monsters and Raphtalia is starting to prove her worth. He also treats her very well for a slave within Melromarc by treating her with medicine, providing good food and comforting her whenever she experiences panic attacks. However, she still has anxiety coming from when her parents were killed in the waves.
Eventually, while monster hunting they come across rabbit-like monsters. Raphtalia is afraid to attack it since it will bleed but Naofumi makes her with her slave crest. As they continue to level up, Raphtalia starts to mature mentally and physically since demi-humans mature with levels. They eventually go back to the weapons shop and upgrade Raphtalia's equipment.. 

** Arc 3: Second Wave **
As Naofumi and Raphtalia start to level up, they start to become stronger and Naofumi's collection of shields and powers expands. Eventually, they make their wave into a cave where they are confronted by a two-headed, black dog. This triggers Raphtalia's PTSD since a monster similar to this is what killed her father and mother. However, with Naofumi's help she is able to overcome her fear and kill the monster.
The two then make their way to Erhard's shop. With Raphtalia maturing as she levels up Erhard notes that she has turned into a fine lady. However Naofumi still thinks of her as a small child. Raphtalia also starts looking out for Naofumi by demanding that he gets better equipment and getting the cheapest meal like he does. They then head to the Dragon Hourglass to see when the next wave is.
At the dragon's hourglass they meet Motoyasu, Ren and Itsuki who have significantly powered-up. The confrontation is awkward and Motoyasu tries to warn Raphtalia about Malty's allegations against Naofumi. Soon after, all of the heroes and their companions are teleported to the location of the wave. While Motoyasu, Ren and Itsuki immediately head to where the main boss is, Naofumi heads to the nearby village to protect the villagers. While there, many of the Melromarc knights cast destructive spells indifferent whether Naofumi was in the area or not. Eventually, the other heroes defeat the Chimera boss and the wave ends. Thanks solely to Naofumi's efforts Riyute village was saved from destruction.
All four of the heroes then attended an after-party celebrating the victory against the waves. However, seeing that Naofumi was starting to get back on his feet, Malty and King Aultcray manipulate Motoyasu into having a duel with Naofumi. If Naofumi loses, Raphtalia will be "freed" and stripped of her slave crest. The two fight and Naofumi surprisingly starts to have an upper hand. However, Malty interferes in the fight and Motoyasu wins. Naofumi, thinking that he lost his only trusted companion started hating the world and unlocked the wrath tree of the curse series. However, Raphtalia still cares for him and comforts him. Naofumi realizes that he has a true companion - one that stays with him just for who he is. With his heart partially mended from Malty's deception, Naofumi realizes he can actually taste for the first time.
Afterwards, the four heroes are assembled to receive their rewards for their efforts during the waves. While Motoyasu, Ren and Itsuki get a significant amount of gold, Naofumi gets scraps. While leaving, Raphtalia suggests that she gets her slave crest against. Naofumi initially doesn't see the point but eventually accepts and they go to the slave trader. 

** Arc 4: Filo Rial **
Being finished at the castle, Naofumi and Raphtalia head to the slave trader's tent to get Raphtalia's slave crest reapplied. Seeing Raphtalia's development under Naofumi, the slave trader offers to buy her back but Naofumi declines. On the way out they see a monster egg gatcha game and Naofumi decides to buy one. While doing his usual business in Castle Town, Naofumi is thanked by the Medicine Shop Owner and the Magic Shop Owner since Naofumi saved their families at Riyute Village during the wave. They give him gifts to help him on his travels.
While in Riyute Village, the gatcha egg hatched and Naofumi names the filolial Filo. While levelling up in the area, Filo grows incredibly rapidly and could be ridden within a day of being hatched. During that time Motoyasu and Malty show up at Riyute Village. They declare Motoyasu the new lord and lays extremely heavy taxes. Naofumi apposes them and, with the help of the ninja-like shadows, is eventually challenged to a race with Motoyasu for the village. During the race, Motoyasu's side cheats but Naofumi is able to win thanks to Filo's incredible power. The village then awards Naofumi a carriage a bill of passage to help his peddling operation.
One morning, Naofumi wakes up to find "Filo" missing and a young naked girl taking Filo's place. He finds out from the slave trader the the young girl is Filo which turns out to be a filolial queen which has transformation abilities. Naofumi attempts to get clothes which won't be destroyed when Filo transforms but it turns out that he needs a magic crystal to create the magic thread. He goes to the Alchemist's Cave with the Magic Shop Owner and finds the crystal after defeating a Nue. The Tailor then makes Filo clothes that work with her transformation abilities. Naofumi, Raphtalia and Filo then start their travels in earnest as a travelling merchant. 

** Arc 5: Peddling **
Taking the carriage and bill of passage as their reward from Riyute Village, Naofumi starts his peddling enterprise in earnest. One of his first jobs is to deliver special medicine to an old lady who immediately recovers. As Naofumi peddles his medicine, he hears rumours about the other heroes and his party start to develop their magical powers. During one of his trips, Naofumi saves an accessory dealer who teaches him how to cast magic for himself and also how to craft accessories. Upon departing with the accessory dealer, the merchant tells Naofumi about a business opportunity in Lerno Village.

Naofumi, Raphtalia and Filo arrive at the village to find it overrun with vines and plant-based monsters. They learn that Motoyasu gave them a "miracle seed" to help them with their famine. One the hero left, the plant grew out of control and started taking over the village. Naofumi sells them weed killer and is eventually commissioned to defeat the BioPlant. Due to their quick thinking, the party is able to defeat the main plant which then kills all of the surrounding vines and monsters. Naofumi then takes seeds from the monster plant and mutates them so that they can be a viable crop-producing plant. The party takes a large amount of the crop as payment.

Next, Naofumi and his party head to Mirso Village which they hear needs medicine. The Shield Hero finds out that Ren had recently slain a dragon in the area and guesses that the dragon's corpse is causing the sickness. Naofumi and his party go to clean up the corpse but the corpse reawakens as the Zombie Dragon. Filo is consumed by the dragon and Naofumi falls into despair. Learning about the Shield of Wrath, he uses it to defeat the dragon but accidentally curses Raphtalia after being overwhelmed by the shield's rage. Filo bursts out of the Zombie Dragon and gives Naofumi a shard form the dragon's core. It seems that the party need to go back to Castle Town since it is the only source of pure holy water to treat Raphtalia's wounds.

On the way back, they meet Melty who quickly becomes friends with Filo. Naofumi agrees to escort her home to Castle Town. Upon reaching Castle Town, the group upgrade their equipment at Erhard's shop and Naofumi learns that Raphtalia and Filo need to class-up in order to level-up any further. 
** Arc 6: Third Wave **
Naofumi and his party had returned to Castle Town to treat Raphtalia's curse and to return Melty back home. However, Motoyasu sees Naofumi and attempts to attack him in the middle of the city. As Naofumi is about to be forced into a fight, Melty steps in, reveals herself as the heir to the Melromarc throne and stops the fight. She is successful but after Naofumi learned that she is related to Malty and King Aultcray, he refused to listen to her. During this time, one of Melromarc's soldiers finds Naofumi and asks to join his party during the fight against the waves. Naofumi initially refuses and asks the soldier to gather a significant amount of money before he can be accepted.

Naofumi and his party then head to the dragon hourglass to conduct a class-up for Raphtalia and Filo. However, they are rejected by the members of The Church of the Three Heroes there. Instead, the group decides to upgrade their equipment.

Before the third wave begins, Naofumi and his party head to a northern territory which has been struck a famine. While there they find that Itsuki had just recently overthrown a corrupt governor. However, the territory had suffered from Itsuki's actions and is experiencing extreme poverty. Naofumi provides a BioPlant seed to help relieve the famine. Before the wave begins, Naofumi runs into Ren and Itsuki who accuse him of stealing rewards for the quests from the Adventurer's Guild. However, Naofumi is able to convince that he is not the one who did it. Afterwards, the Melromarc soldier comes to Naofumi with the money Naofumi had asked for. Naofumi tells the soldier to use the money to improve his equipment and welcomes him to the party. The wave begins and the group is teleported to the wave.

As before, Naofumi and his party defends the nearby village. However, even after a long while the wave still hadn't been defeated. Naofumi leaves some people behind to defend the village and investigates. He finds that the three heroes had been fighting over their different "game knowledge" and were unable to find the wave boss. Naofumi chastises them and finds the Soul Eater wave boss who ends up being too powerful for the other three heroes. Naofumi desperately uses his curse series skill to defeat it however the wave does not disappear. Then, a mysterious woman called Glass appears and challenges the heroes. She quickly overpowers even Naofumi however the timer for the wave finishes and Glass is forced to withdraw before the dimensional rifts close.

The next day at the castle, Naofumi is forced to appear before the king who asks how Naofumi got so strong. Naofumi only agrees to tell if the king bows down to king. Furiously, the king ejects Naofumi from the castle. 

** Arc 7: Devil of the Shield **
Leaving the castle, Naofumi tries to figure out how to get Raphtalia and Filo to class-up since the church in Melromarc is refusing to help. With Erhard's advice and assistance, Naofumi decides to go to Shieldfreeden to get the ceremony complete. On the road, they are intercepted by Melty and her escort. Melty attempts to get Naofumi to apologize and talk to the king but Naofumi refuses. During their discussion, Melty's escort suddenly attempts to kill Melty while using a magical recording device. Naofumi saves Melty but some of the escorts escape.

Soon after the rescue, the party enter the town to find wanted posters for themselves and doctored footage which show them as the villains. With the whole nation on alert, they decide to sneak all the way to Shieldfreeden. During the trip, Melty proves her competence and the group is helped with their few allies along the way. Eventually the other heroes with Malty and a hoard of Melromarc soldiers catch up with them. While Motoyasu, Ren and Itsuki attempt to take Naofumi's party captive, Malty straight up tries to kill them. Thanks to interference from the Queen's ninja-like shadows, Naofumi's party is able to escape.

After escaping, one of the shadows introduces herself to Naofumi and asks him to change direction and move towards Siltvelt to meet the Queen. Seeing no other choice, Naofumi accepts and the group moves in the direction opposite of Shieldfreeden. On the way, they are hunted by Motoyasu and Malty but eventually escape. Upon Melty's suggestion, they meet up with a nearby, friendly noble who provides shelter for them. However, soon after, a neighboring noble aligned with The Church of the Three Heroes captures the friendly noble on suspicions of hiding the "Devil of the Shield". To protect Naofumi, Melty also lets herself be captured by Idol Rabier. While hidden, Raphtalia reveals that this terrible noble used to own her as a slave and loved to torture demi-humans.

Naofumi, Raphtalia and Filo decide to save Melty and the friendly noble. Raphtalia is able to overcome her trauma regarding Rabier but he accidentally falls out of his own window and is presumed dead. Naofumi and his group head to the dungeon where the noble kept all of his tortured slaves. They free the slaves and Raphtalia finds both Keel and the deceased Rifana who used to live in the same village. However, the dying Rabier released the sealed Tyrant Dragon Rex which starts devastating the area. Naofumi's party lures it away from the nearby civilians and attempt to fight the monster. When it seemed hopeless, the filolial queen Fitoria appeared and defeated the beast. She then takes Naofumi's party to her territory.

While in her territory, Fitoria threatens Naofumi that if he doesn't unite the Heroes, she will have to kill them all so that more useful heroes can be summoned in their place. She then proceeds to challenge Filo to battle with Melty as hostage. Filo is able to impress Fitoria, who then crowns her as the next filolial queen. Fitoria then teleports Naofumi and his party close to Motoyasu to make amends.

As soon as Motoyasu sees Naofumi, he tries to kill him. According to the Spear Hero, Ren and Itsuki were killed by Naofumi. Naofumi was able to repel Motoyasu when they were both suddenly targeted by an immensely powerful attack. The pope of The Church of the Three Heroes reveals himself and attacks the two "fake heroes". It seems hopeless for Naofumi and Motoyasu until they are reinforced by Ren and Itsuki who were actually saved from the pope's ambush by shadows. The four heroes fight together and seem to be on roughly equal grounds until Queen Mirellia shows up to stop the pope. Naofumi uses one of his Wrath Series cursed abilities to defeat the pope but is nearly killed in the process. 

** Arc 8: Punishment **
Naofumi wakes up in the royal Melromarc hospital after passing out from using a cursed skill against the pope. The Queen welcomes him back and updates him on recent events. With her return, Malty and King Aultcray's abuse of power and discrimination against Naofumi had been revealed to the Queen. King Aultcray has been dethroned and Queen Mirellia will hold a trial for both.

During the trial, a slave crest is placed on Malty so that she could not lie. She is forced to reveal that she summoned all of the Heroes to Melromarc so that other countries could not have their own heroes. She also falsely accused Naofumi of raping her when she wasn't even a virgin, tried to take Raphtalia away from him in a dirty duel, and attempted to kill her younger sister Melty for the throne. The King seems to have followed her word just because she is his beloved daughter. Thus after three long months, the civilians, nobles, knights, servants, ministers, Motoyasu, Ren, Itsuki, and their party members finally learned the truth. Naofumi never tried to rape Malty at the start of his adventure, it was all a cleverly devised lie. The Shield Hero was innocent of everything, the victim of an evil conspiracy devised by both the King and the First Princess. All of their plot and evil deeds were exposed in front of everyone. Although Aultcray admits that his actions were unbecoming of royalty, he insists that Naofumi is the "Shield Devil" and claims to have done all this "for the sake of Melromarc and his family" so that the misfortune of his past didn't repeat. The Queen leaves the fate of the King and the First Princess in Naofumi's hands and, recalling his suffering at their hands, he sentences them both to death.

At the execution, Malty and Aultcray are placed in the stocks and locked in, and as the Queen prepares to give the signal, the swordsmen get ready to cut the ropes. Malty pleads for Motoyasu to help her, but he simply turns his face away from her in complete disgust, horrifying her that not even the man she manipulated the most is going to spare her life. Desperate to be allowed to live, Malty turns to the only person left she thinks can show her mercy: The one person she's wronged the most, the Shield Hero, Naofumi - for the first time calling him by his given name, "Naofumi-sama" - and begs him for help. Her Slave Crest doesn't react, making Naofumi realize that her pleading is sincere, and cementing his view that this would not be a satisfying death for her. Upon also seeing that even the queen herself is very reluctant and sad about going ahead with the execution, Naofumi suddenly stops the execution and demands that Aultcray be renamed to "Trash" and Malty be renamed to "Bitch" (with her adventurer name being "Whore" instead of "Myne"); such humiliation overjoys everyone. Angered, the fallen king tried to attack the Shield Hero but was stopped at sword point by his former guards. Thus, it became clear that Trash's authority was ended. To add insult to injury, he and Bitch were forced to bow in supplication before the hero they'd hated and wronged, asking for his mercy. This drew a massive laugh from the spectators, leaving Trash and Bitch's humiliation complete. The two were stripped of their royal status and ejected from the castle.

Later, in a private discussion with Naofumi, the queen reveals that significant political unrest was created by Bitch summoning all four heroes since usually the heroes are spread among the nation. She also reveals that it was her actions that led him to Raphtalia, though not directly. Mirellia wanted the slave trader to sell Naofumi a slave and asked her Shadows to arrange for it. However, she didn't specify whom to arrange and was surprised when a demi-human of the Seaetto territory became the Shield Hero's first party member. Naofumi reaffirms that he will continue to work with Melromarc.

After that, Naofumi and his party go the Melromarc's dragon hourglass. With the Church of the Three Heroes disbanded and replaced with a new religion, the Church of the Four Heroes, Raphtalia and Filo are finally able to get their class-up. However, they were not able to choose their class-up path since the cow-lick which Fitoria gave Filo interfered with the ceremony. Despite not liking this, Naofumi decides to keep this class-ups. 

** Arc 9: Cal Mira Island **
Hearing from the queen that there is an event at Cal Mira Archipelago which will boost experience gain, Naofumi and his party head to the docks go to the area. However, on the way Raphtalia asks to go by her home village which was destroyed during the first wave. While there they meet L'Arc and Therese who are two adventurers also going to Cal Mira.

After arriving in Cal Mira, Naofumi and his party get a tour with the Earl of Habenburg while the other heroes recover from their seasickness. Naofumi also finds a magical tablet which teaches him a powerful buff spell. The next day Naofumi and his party start levelling up across the islands. Eventually, they party up with L'Arc and Therese and work well with them. During their levelling up, L'Arc is revealed to have a weapon very similar to the Legendary Weapons but Naofumi doesn't ask about it. Eventually, Naofumi and his party level up to a point where they are hardly gaining experience so they decide to enjoy the islands instead.

When swimming in the ocean, Filo finds an underwater dragon hourglass which states that there will be a wave at the archipelago in only a few days. Naofumi warns the heroes and the queen and everyone prepares to defend against the wave. During the fight against the wave, L'Arc and Therese prove to be incredibly powerful and even defeat the wave boss in a single hit. However, once they defeated the boss, they turn on Naofumi. Apparently, they must kill all Legendary Heroes of Raphtalia's World to save their own world. Surprisingly, L'Arc proves to be an honorable and extremely competent fighter. The two groups seem evenly matched until Glass arrives to back up L'Arc and Therese. Naofumi seems cornered until Rishia and the queen back him up. They detonate a barrel of rucolu fruit which instantly intoxicates anyone in the area - except for Naofumi who is immune to intoxication. Seeing that Glass is intoxicated and unable to fight, the three other-worlders retreat through the wave. After the fight, it becomes clear the the other three heroes are incredibly weak.

In the aftermath of the wave, Naofumi decides that he needs another party member. One day he finds Motoyasu trying to calm a trying Rishia. It seems that Itsuki became jealous of Rishia for becoming useful in the Cal Mira wave and his party members framed her for doing something she didn't do. She was ejected from the party. Seeing the correlation with his own story, Naofumi tries to confront Itsuki but the Bow Hero wouldn't apologies and let her back in his party. Rishia nearly kills herself until she is saved by Filo. Naofumi offers to make her stronger and invites her into his own party. 

** Arc 10: Intermission **
With the pope defeated, Queen Mirellia prepares a feast to celebrate. During the celebration, she mentions that the Cal Mira Archipelago event will activate soon which will greatly increase the heroes' levels. She also attempts to get the four Legendary Heroes to cooperate with each other.

After the celebration, the four heroes meet together to share information with the queen moderating. Motoyasu, Ren and Itsuki are convinced that Naofumi is cheating with the curse series shield. When they discuss their own power-up methods, the discussion quickly turns into a fight since the three heroes have vastly different methods. However, Naofumi took notes of what each heroes' power-up methods were.

Later on at night, Naofumi tried believing in the other heroes' power-up systems. Suddenly, all of the power-up methods started appearing in Naofumi's shield. He took the night experimenting and applying what he could. The next morning Naofumi tried to tell the other heroes that all power-up methods work but they wouldn't listen. Before heading to Cal Mira, Naofumi stops by the Erhard's shop. To the old man's despair, Naofumi was able to copy all of the shield which he had made. However, Naofumi promises to keep on shopping at Erhard's shop. Naofumi and his party then make their way to the harbor town. 

** Arc 11: Spirit Turtle **
In the aftermath of the fight with the other-worldly heroes it becomes painfully clear how the Heroes need to become stronger. Naofumi is given access to Melromarc's stockpile to use the different power-up methods and Queen Mirellia arranges for combat advisors to train the four heroes. The heroes are introduced to Eclair Seaetto, (a sword wielder who now governs the area around Raphtalia's Village) and Elrasla Ragnarok, the last practitioner of the Hengen Muso combat style. Even though these combat advisors are shown to be effective and Eclair even beats Ren in a duel despite not being a hero, Ren, Itsuki and Motoyasu try to skip out of the training sessions. However, Naofumi's team - especially Rishia - start to learn about Hengen Muso. With the Heroes repeated trying to skip out of the training sessions, the queen negotiates with them and allows them to leave if they investigate rumours about mysterious attacks around the world.

Naofumi and his party are eventually assigned by the Queen to also look into these rumours. They head to Lerno Village and find it overrun by parasitic familiars. Naofumi's team is able to fend off these familiars however Keel is almost taken over by their parasitic abilities. Back at the castle, Melromarc's shadows have gained enough information to find out that the mysterious beast on the rampage is a Guardian Beast called the Spirit Tortoise and the monsters attacking around the world are its familiars. Naofumi and his party hurriedly try to rush to an eastern country where the Spirit Tortoise resides.

Arriving in the eastern country, Naofumi finds the surrounding area in utter devastation. Many people have died and the three other heroes have gone missing. Attempting to stop the Spirit Tortoise in its tracks, Naofumi, Raphtalia, Filo, Eclair and the Hengen Muso lady attack. After a hard-fought battle, the Spirit Tortoise is beheaded and the defence-force celebrates their victory. Climbing onto the back of the Spirit Tortoise, they find a temple with a warning from the previous hero about the Spirit Tortoise. Naofumi then starts the search for the missing heroes were investigating into the Spirit Tortoise.

While fighting a hoard of left-over familiars, Naofumi and his party are confronted by Ost who claims to be a familiar of the Spirit Tortoise. She says that the Guardian Beast is not dead and it has been corrupted by some unknown force. Soon after, they find that the Spirit Tortoise has been regenerated with a new, terrifying form and is now headed straight for Melromarc. Naofumi and his party attempt to slay the Spirit Tortoise or at least attempt to slow it down but all efforts seem useless. Eventually, Fitoria arrived to help in the battle. She agrees to fight the main body of the Spirit Tortoise while Naofumi and a portion of the defence force infiltrates into the interior of the body. They determine that they need to destroy the head and the heart at the same time to truly defeat the Spirit Tortoise. After a failed attempt and a harrowing effort, they are able to destroy the head and seal the heart at the same time to stop the Spirit Tortoise for good. Naofumi and his party travel further into the Spirit Tortoise to find who has been corrupting and manipulating this Guardian Beast.

Entering the core chamber, they find the other three heroes captured and the Spirit Tortoise's energy being siphoned by a villain calling himself Kyo. When it seems like Kyo was too powerful to be defeat, L'Arc, Therese and Glass show up to also defeat the villain. The two groups work together and fight the evil Vassal Book wielder. It is only because of Ost helping Naofumi cast Liberation-class magic and Rishia hidden abilities the heroes were able to defeat Kyo and destroy the Spirit Tortoise core. Kyo takes the remaining energy gathered by the Spirit Tortoise and then flees through a portal to his own world. However, with the Spirit Tortoise's core destroyed, Ost (the spirit of the Spirit Tortoise) starts to fade away. She gives a solemn goodbye with Naofumi's party and then asks them to return the Spirit Tortoise's energy back to their world. If they are successful, this will increase the time before the next wave. As a final gift, Ost gives Naofumi a special exception as a Legendary Hero which allows him to travel between worlds. With her death, Naofumi, his party and L'Arc's party travel through the portal to try to capture Kyo. 

** Arc 12: Rebuilding Arc Part 1 **
Naofumi and his party arrive back to their world from Glass' World. They release the Spirit Tortoise energy which increases the time until the next wave by a few months. As a reward for helping with the Spirit Tortoise catastrophe, Naofumi decides to become the lord of the territory which contains Raphtalia's Village. Naofumi asks the Slave Trader to obtain as many slaves that used to live in this territory as he can.

Heading to the village, Naofumi finds that no progress has been done in the rebuilding efforts despite it being months since the wave which destroyed it. It turns out that Eclair, the governor of this territory would rather train then work on rebuilding efforts. He asks Melty to help assist in properly governing the area. Naofumi then uses his BioPlant to help get food for the village and employs various monsters to help with the labour. He divides tasks among the villagers to maintain the village and generate revenue. Soon after, the Slave Trader arrives and explains that since the "Shield Hero" is attempting to obtain all of the original Lurolona villagers, the slave market for them has gone incredibly expensive. He then recommends Naofumi to go the Zeltoble where most of the slaves are apparently kept. The village is also attacked by slave hunters who want to obtain their own Lurolona slaves.

Naofumi eventually goes to Zeltoble and finds a few of his business partners like the Accessory Merchant. He is told that an efficient way to generate large sums of money is to fight in the coliseum battles. Naofumi and his party agree to become part of the team battles with Naofumi's business partners supporting him. Their team is incredibly successful and they eventually are paired to fight against "Nadia" - a favourite among the coliseum patrons. Naofumi's team has an incredibly difficult fight with Sadeena until Raphtalia recognizes her as the "big sister" of her original village - Sadeena. When Sadeena finds out that she is fighting against Raphtalia, she agrees to fake her defeat.

Unfortunately, the coliseum patrons are incredibly unhappy with Sadeena's defeat and the coliseum arranges an immediate second fight against their top fighter - Murder Pierrot. However, together they are able to defeat this difficult foe. With the Accessory Deal and Slave trader's backing, they are able to confront the crooked businessman arranging the fights and have Sadeena's slave crest removed. It is also revealed that while Naofumi and his team were trying to buy Lurolona slaves, Sadeena was trying to do the same. Naofumi then transport all of them back to their home village.

After returning the villagers back to their village, Naofumi peruses the Zeltoble Slave Trader's stock. He finds a deathly sick Hakuko demi-human and her brother. He decides to buy the two slaves named Fohl and Atla and treat Atla's illness. Over time, Atla's illness (which seemed to have no cure for the two siblings) slowly started to fade away. Both Fohl and Atla reaffirmed they loyalty to Naofumi. He then takes them to Castle Town in order to reset their levels and they come across Trash who has a bizarre reaction upon seeing them. Apparently, these two demi-humans are the children of his long-lost sister and the son of his bitter enemy. Around this point Murder Pierrot arrives in Naofumi's village but Naofumi turns her away. Queen Melromarc also asks Naofumi to capture Motoyasu who has run amok. 

** Arc 13: Rebuilding Arc Part 2 **
Realizing that there are still powerful enemies besides the other heroes, Naofumi and his party train in earnest. Their main efforts lie in learning the powerful Hengen Muso style which allows them to utilize life force. Naofumi also stops by Erhard's shop to order materials for Raphtalia's Vassal Katana.

Returning to the village, Naofumi finds that an eccentric alchemist named Rat had arrived. She offers her services which allow her to research and modify different monsters which Naofumi accepts. She modifies Naofumi's BioPlant in order to creating housing for the village. Apparently, the village had also received a large amount of gifts from Shield-loving countries such as Siltvelt or Shieldfreeden and they find a dragon egg among them. With Rat and Wyndia's help, Naofumi decides to raise it and he is named Gaelion.

Soon after, Naofumi returns to Erhard's shop and the Vassal Katana gains a new ability from the weapon Erhard had forged.
