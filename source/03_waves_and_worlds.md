*Source: shield-hero.fandom.com (Waves of Calamity, Guardian Beasts, Four
Legendary Weapons, Army/Vanguards of the Waves) + GameRant/CBR cross-checks.
Paraphrased.*

## What the Waves actually are
The **Waves of Calamity** are recurring disasters that tear dimensional rifts and
pour monsters into the land. Deeper truth: they happen because **separate worlds
are being forced to fuse together**. When two worlds collide, the "weaker" one is
at risk of being absorbed/erased by the "stronger." Heroes fight the Wave monsters
and seal the rifts to stop the merge. The Legendary Weapons exist specifically to
resist this fusion by summoning suitable champions.

A Dragon Hourglass shows the **countdown** to the next regional Wave and warps the
registered heroes (and party) into it when it begins.

## The grand cause (late-story reveal)
The Waves are not a natural disaster — they are **engineered by a hostile deity,
Medea Pideth Machina**, who is deliberately merging multiple worlds (the final arc
puts it at **eight worlds**) for her own ends. The world-fusion history explains
why each world has parallel Legendary/Vassal systems: worlds have merged before
(e.g., a shield+bow world fused with a sword+spear world, etc.).

## The connected worlds
- **Raphtalia's World** — Naofumi's summoning world: the continent of Melromarc,
  Siltvelt, Faubrey, Zeltoble, Q'ten Lo, Shieldfrieden, Rock Valley. Home to
  Raphtalia, Filo, Melty, the Guardian Beasts.
- **Kizuna's / Glass's World** — a parallel world with its own four Legendary
  Heroes and Vassal Heroes (Glass, L'Arc, Ethnobalt, S'yne, etc.). Because of the
  fusion logic, its heroes initially try to **kill Naofumi's world's heroes** to
  spare their own world — making them early antagonists before becoming allies
  against the true enemy.

## Guardian Beasts (Raphtalia's World)
Colossal sealed protector-beasts meant to defend the world by gathering energy for
a barrier **if the Heroes fail to stop the Waves**. They are a fail-safe — and a
catastrophe if awakened wrongly. Known ones:
- **Spirit Tortoise** — a mountain-sized turtle; its familiar **Ost** is its
  human face. Prematurely hijacked by **Kyo** to rampage and harvest souls →
  the central crisis of the Spirit Tortoise arc.
- **Phoenix** — a paired firebird Guardian; the battle where **Atla** dies.
- **Kirin** — a lightning Guardian (defeated single-handedly by Takt, revealing
  his threat level).
- **Dragon King** — the fourth Guardian Beast.

## The antagonist organization
- **Vanguards of the Waves** — humans **reincarnated** into other worlds by
  "God"/Medea, picked for selfish/negative traits and given powers to **steal hero
  weapons** and **resurrect** as long as their soul survives. If they try to expose
  the organization, their body/soul is destroyed. They *want* the Waves to succeed.
  Members include **Kyo**, **Takt**, **Malty** (later), **Hidemasa**, **Mald**, and
  **S'yne's sister**.
- **Army of the Waves** — the broader hostile force blamed for the Waves and for
  destroying historical records and martial traditions across worlds.

## Why this matters for the rebuild
Two hooks are mechanically perfect for the bot:
1. **A Wave countdown** = a natural "doom clock" (tension that fills and detonates
   into a Wave event).
2. **Reputation/standing** = a core, evolving stat (the Shield Hero's despised →
   respected arc; demi-human vs human politics).
