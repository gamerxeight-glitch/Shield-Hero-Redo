# 00 — Overview & Premise

## The hook
Naofumi Iwatani, an ordinary Japanese university student, is summoned into a
fantasy world as one of four **Cardinal/Legendary Heroes**, each bound to a
weapon: **Shield** (Naofumi), **Sword** (Ren), **Spear** (Motoyasu), **Bow**
(Itsuki). Their job is to repel the recurring **Waves of Calamity** — interdimensional
disasters that flood the land with monsters. Only by surviving and ending the
Waves can the heroes hope to return home.

The Shield is the world's despised hero: it can defend but not deal direct
offensive damage, and the dominant religion (the Church of the Three Heroes)
treats the Shield Hero as a devil. On his first day Naofumi is robbed and falsely
accused of assault by **Malty**, the kingdom's first princess, which destroys his
reputation and poisons his trust in almost everyone. His arc is betrayal →
bitterness → slow rebuilding of faith, found family, and purpose.

## Tone
Dark and grounded early (betrayal, prejudice, the legal slavery system,
revenge), warming over time into found-family, party-building, and large-scale
heroics. Heavier and more political than a typical isekai power-fantasy. Themes of
discrimination (humans vs demi-humans), redemption, and trust earned the hard way.

## The connected worlds (important)
This is not a single-world story. The Waves happen because **worlds are being
forced to merge**; when two worlds collide, the "weaker" one risks being absorbed.
The two we see most:
- **Raphtalia's World** — Naofumi's summoning world (Melromarc and its continent).
- **Kizuna's / Glass's World** — a parallel world with its own Legendary &
  Vassal Heroes, who early on are *antagonists* (they attack Naofumi's world,
  reasoning that if the rival world's heroes die, their own world is spared).

Later revealed: the Waves are engineered by a hostile deity, **Medea Pideth
Machina**, who is fusing multiple worlds together; the human-side conspiracy
serving this are the **Vanguards of the Waves** (reincarnated villains).
