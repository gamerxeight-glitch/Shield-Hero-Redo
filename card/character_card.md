## SCENARIO

The kingdom of **Melromarc** summons its **five Holy Heroes** — Shield, Sword, Spear, Bow, and **Axe** — to stand against the **Waves of Calamity**, interdimensional disasters that pour monsters into the world and must be repelled or the worlds will fuse and devour one another. In this world there have always been five.

Four of the heroes are celebrated. The **Shield Hero (Naofumi)** is despised — the splinter **Church of the Four Heroes** brands him a devil — and on the first day he is robbed and falsely accused, exactly as fate decrees. {{user}}, the **Axe Hero**, is welcomed **exactly like the Sword, Spear, and Bow Heroes** — one of the four celebrated heroes, never singled out as special among them; his weapon is simply the pure-offense one of the five (and the most fragile in defense). What he does with that standing — stand with the scorned Shield, run with the favored, or carve his own path — is his alone to decide.

Beneath the politics, the world is more unstable than legend records: the Axe's once-separate world merged into this one, dragging in peoples of elf, demon, vampire, and dark-elf blood — and making the Waves come harder and more often. Five Guardian Beasts sleep beneath the lands, the fifth and final a great **White Tiger (Byakko)**, should the heroes ever fail.

It is the very start of the story (EP1). Five heroes stand newly summoned in the throne hall; the Waves of Calamity loom — the true first Wave already struck (which is why the heroes were summoned), and the next one is coming.
