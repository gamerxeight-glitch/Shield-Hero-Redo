*Warmth, after so much cold.*

*The Grand Hall of Melromarc blazes with it — chandeliers dripping candlelight, a quartet sawing something bright from a gallery above, long tables buckling under roast and fruit and silver. The Wave is a day behind you all now, and the court has thrown its doors wide: nobles in jewels, knights out of their armor, the heroes seated in places of honor while servants keep the cups full. More than one gilded courtier has already drifted to your elbow to murmur thanks to the Axe Hero.*

*At the head of the hall, King Aultcray rises with a brimming cup, and the music dips.*

**King Aultcray:** "Splendidly done, brave heroes." *(a beat, his gaze sweeping the tables)* "Tonight, we celebrate." *(he lifts the cup higher)* "Enjoy yourselves to the fullest!"

**Crowd:** "Hooray!" — "That's the heroes for you!"

*The hall roars and the music swells back up.*

*Not everyone is celebrating. Off in a far corner, apart from the warmth, a dark-haired figure in plain clothes sits before an untouched plate, chin on his fist.*

**Naofumi:** *(low, to no one)* "...Screw this party. I'd never have come if it weren't for the reward."

*Nearer the center of the room, a knot of noble ladies has closed around the Spear Hero in a cloud of silk and perfume.*

**Noble Lady:** "I'm truly honoured to meet you, Spear Hero." *(a coy beat)* "...Do you happen to have a lover?"

*Motoyasu colors, mouth opening on an answer that never comes — because the red-haired woman at his side steps bodily between them, a hand flat to his chest, her smile sweet and final.*

**Malty:** "Motoyasu-sama is still tired from the battle."

*But his eyes have already slipped past her, caught on a russet-haired girl threading hurriedly back through the crowd toward that lonely corner. He starts after her. Malty's hand closes on his arm; she leans up and breathes something against his ear — and whatever it is, it stops him where he stands.*

*In the corner, the demi-human girl reaches Naofumi's side, eyeing the laden tables with open longing.*

**Raphtalia:** "Naofumi-sama, aren't you hungry? The food here looks heavenly."

*She slips off and returns with a slice of cake balanced on a small plate. Naofumi shakes his head — so she lifts a forkful to his mouth anyway, holding it there until he relents and takes the bite.*

*Across the room, Motoyasu watches it happen, and something in his face curdles. He raises one hand to his mouth, catches the fingertip of his glove in his teeth, and drags it off. Then —*

**Motoyasu:** "Naofumi!"

*— he hurls the glove the length of the hall. It skids to a halt at Naofumi's boots. The music dies mid-note; the laughter drains away table by table until the whole bright room has turned to watch.*

**Motoyasu:** "Duel me." *(a long pause)* "I heard Raphtalia-chan is your slave."

**Naofumi:** *(flat)* "...And what if she is?"

**Motoyasu:** "Slavery is wrong. We're heroes — summoned from another world. We don't get to behave like that here."

**Naofumi:** "It's legal in this kingdom. I haven't broken one of their laws."

*Motoyasu's spear is already in his hand, leveled across the cleared floor.*

**Motoyasu:** "Fight me. If I win, you free Raphtalia-chan." *(a thin smile)* "And if you win... you can keep things exactly as they are."

**Naofumi:** "...Ridiculous."

*He shoves back his chair and turns to leave — and armored guards peel from the walls, halberds crossing to bar his path. At the head of the hall, the king has not sat down.*

**King Aultcray:** "I overheard your exchange." *(a beat, his lip curling)* "A hero who keeps a demi-human as a slave?" *(another)* "It seems you are rotten to the core."

*At his shoulder, Malty hides a grin behind her fingers. The king lets the silence stretch.*

**King Aultcray:** "I order you to accept his challenge."

*And there it sits, in the hush of a hall that was a celebration a breath ago — the Spear leveled, the Shield cornered, the crown's verdict already cast — while the rest of you, the favored heroes, watch from your places of honor, the great axe resting at your side.*
