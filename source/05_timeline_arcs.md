# 05 — Timeline / Arcs (paraphrased)

A chronological spine of the story, with anime-season mapping and notes on where
the web novel (WN), light novel (LN), manga, and anime diverge. Useful later for
"season/arc gating" in the bot.
*Source: Wikipedia (volumes list, TV series article) + shield-hero.fandom.com arc
& volume synopses. Paraphrased; arc names are descriptive.*

## Continuity map (high level)
- **Web Novel (WN):** the original, completed first; broadest/most divergent late.
- **Light Novel (LN):** expanded retelling (illustrations by Minami Seira); begins
  to **diverge from the WN around vols 8–9** and is **fully divergent by ~vol 16**;
  ~22 main volumes (+ side volumes). Spin-off: *The Reprise of the Spear Hero*.
- **Anime:** 3 seasons so far, adapting the LN spine (with its own small changes,
  e.g. how Ren's curse penalty works, Keel's reveal timing).

## Arc-by-arc spine

### Arc 1 — Summoning & Betrayal (Anime S1 opening)
The four heroes are summoned to Melromarc. Naofumi is isolated, then robbed and
falsely accused by Malty on day one; the kingdom turns on him. He hits rock bottom.

### Arc 2 — Raphtalia (rebuilding)
Desperate to have any "sword," Naofumi buys the traumatized slave Raphtalia. As she
heals and grows, she becomes his partner; he begins clawing back competence and a
sliver of trust. Early Waves are fought.

### Arc 3 — Filo & the Carriage
Naofumi hatches Filo, who becomes a Filolial Queen. The party becomes a traveling
peddler-and-protector trio; Naofumi quietly builds goodwill among commoners while
still officially despised. Introduction of Melty.

### Arc 4 — Three Heroes Church / the Coup (Anime S1 climax)
The other heroes keep failing; the Church, losing influence, frames Naofumi for
harming Princess Melty, then attempts to assassinate the royals and kill all four
heroes, with its High Priest declaring himself a god. Mirellia returns; Naofumi
slays the High Priest (Wrath Shield); the Church is outlawed; Malty & Aultcray are
disgraced and renamed "Bitch"/"Trash." (Glass first appears during a Wave here as
a powerful outsider.)

### Arc 5 — Cal Mira & the Other-World Heroes (Anime S2 setup)
A training trip to the Cal Mira archipelago; Naofumi unknowingly befriends L'Arc &
Therese, who (with Glass) are secretly from the parallel world and turn on him
during the Cal Mira Wave. The "other world" threat becomes clear.

### Arc 6 — Spirit Tortoise (Anime S2)
The Guardian Beast **Spirit Tortoise** rampages, having been hijacked by **Kyo
Ethnina**. It wipes out the other heroes' parties (sending Ren/Itsuki/Motoyasu into
their curse spirals). With **Ost** and **Fitoria**, Naofumi's party destroys its
heart — but Kyo escapes to his own world with stolen energy.

### Arc 7 — Kizuna's World / Hunting Hero (Anime S2 latter half)
Pursuing Kyo, Naofumi is cast into the parallel world's labyrinth, frees **Kizuna**,
and gathers allies (Glass, L'Arc, Ethnobalt, Therese, Raphtalia gains the Katana,
Raph-chan is created). They hunt and kill **Kyo**, freeing the captured souls;
former enemies become allies against the true cause of the Waves.

### Arc 8 — Return, Reform & Reckonings (Anime S3 early)
Back home, Naofumi rebuilds his **village** of freed demi-humans, recruits Fohl &
Atla, Sadeena, Rishia trains up, the fallen heroes (Ren/Itsuki) begin redemption.
Political reform under Mirellia/Melty; demi-human relations improve.

### Arc 9 — Q'ten Lo (Anime S3)
The party travels to isolationist **Q'ten Lo**, uncovering Raphtalia's royal
heritage and the corrupt regime (Makina/puppet-emperor Ruftmilia); Shildina arc;
Naofumi finds the Shield's hidden power-up methods on ancient tablets.

### Arc 10 — Phoenix (Anime S3 climax region)
The Guardian Beast **Phoenix** battle. **Atla** sacrifices herself to save Naofumi,
unlocking the **Blessed Shield of Compassion**. Hints that someone interfered to
provoke the Guardian.

### Arc 11 — Faubrey & Takt
**Takt** reveals himself, steals the Legendary Shield, fatally wounds **Mirellia**,
and declares war. Lent the Seven Star Staff, Naofumi's superior weapon mastery
defeats Takt; the Vanguards' hand becomes undeniable.

### Arc 12+ — Vanguards of the Waves & the Final Cause (LN late arcs)
Escalation into Kizuna's world again (Hidemasa, S'yne's sister, Mald, Malty as
Vanguards), liberation of stolen Vassal weapons, time-travel to the past
(**Mamorou**, the origin of Siltvelt/Fitoria), and the march toward the true enemy
behind the Waves — the deity **Medea Pideth Machina** fusing the eight worlds.

## Anime season shorthand (for quick gating)
- **S1:** Arcs 1–4 (Summoning/Betrayal → Raphtalia → Filo → Three Heroes Church).
- **S2:** Arcs 5–7 (Cal Mira → Spirit Tortoise/Kyo → Kizuna's world).
- **S3:** Arcs 8–11 (village/reform → Q'ten Lo → Phoenix → toward Faubrey/Takt).
- **Beyond anime:** Arc 12+ (Vanguards, the past, Medea) — LN territory.
