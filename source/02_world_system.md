# 02 — World System (paraphrased)

*Source: shield-hero.fandom.com (Status Magic System, Dragon Hourglass, Four
Legendary Weapons, Vassal Weapons, Power-up Methods, Curse Series, Magic) + CBR
cross-checks. Paraphrased.*

## Levels & the Status Magic System
The world runs on a visible, game-like progression: people have levels and stats
that grow by defeating monsters/Waves. Heroes share experience with bonded party
members. Progress hits a **level cap** that must be broken by a **Class-Up**
ceremony.

## Class-Up & the Dragon Hourglass
A **Dragon Hourglass** is a sacred device (often guarded by the Church). When a
Legendary/Vassal/Seven-Star wielder touches one, it **registers** them and gives
a **countdown to the next Wave** in that hourglass's region, and can act as a
**warp point** for heroes. It's also where **Class-Up** is performed: breaking the
level cap and choosing a growth path that biases certain stats. (Plot point: a
"cowlick" from Fitoria once forced an auto-selected, superior class-up path on
Filo and Raphtalia, letting them pass level 40.)

## The weapon hierarchy
Three tiers of "living weapon" spirits that forcibly summon suitable wielders to
resist the world-fusion:

1. **Four Legendary Weapons** — Shield, Sword, Spear, Bow. The top tier; their
   wielders are the Cardinal/Legendary Heroes. (In Naofumi's world the Shield's
   power-up methods were *hidden* by an unknown force — Naofumi finds them on
   tablets in Q'ten Lo.)
2. **Seven Star Weapons** (a.k.a. Vassal weapons of Naofumi's world) — a step
   below the Legendaries: e.g. **Staff** (Aultcray), **Whip** (Takt), **Gauntlet**
   (Fohl), **Harpoon** (Sadeena), **Projectile** (Rishia), **Axe**, **Claw**, and
   the long-lost **Carriage** (Fitoria) — its loss is why the rest are called
   "Seven Star."
3. **Vassal Weapons (Kizuna's world)** — Katana (Raphtalia), Scythe (L'Arc), Fan
   (Glass), Book (Ethnobalt/originally Kyo), Boat, Mirror, Musical Instrument
   (Itsuki), Sewing Set (S'yne), Ofuda (Shildina), Hunting Tool (Kizuna), Chain.

Each world has its own set of Legendary + Vassal heroes; the systems mirror each
other across worlds.

## Power-up methods & Weapon Copy
Hero weapons grow through multiple **power-up methods** (the heroes argue because
each only knows the ones from "their" game). **Weapon Copy:** touching/absorbing a
weapon of a matching class unlocks that form for later use (equip bonuses may need
materials). Mastering *all* the methods together is a key strength gap between
Naofumi and the other (single-method-minded) heroes.

## The Curse Series (Cursed Weapons)
Forms unlocked through intense negative emotion; they grant great power at a steep
price, amplifying the user's worst feelings and often inflicting lasting penalties:
- **Naofumi** — **Wrath Shield** (rage); risks birthing the "Wrath Dragon."
- **Motoyasu** — **Spears of Lust / Envy** (perceives women as pigs).
- **Ren** — **Swords of Greed / Gluttony** (can't gain XP/money while cursed).
- **Itsuki** — **Bow of Pride** (loses his own will).
Recovery means confronting the emotion and rejecting the curse. (Naofumi later
unlocks **Blessed** counterparts, e.g. the **Shield of Compassion** via Atla.)

## Magic
Cast via chant + mana; people have affinities (fire, water, ice, wind, light/dark,
etc.). Heroes can read a "translation"/help text. **Filolials and dragons** have
the highest magic affinity among monsters. Advanced disciplines:
- **Hengen Muso** — a martial art channeling **Life Force** (a separate energy
  from mana/levels), able to overcome raw stat gaps; taught by Elrasla.
- **Liberation**-class support magic — high-tier ritual magic.

## Demi-humans & monsters
- **Demi-humans (Ajin):** animal-traited people; their **bodies mature with
  levels** (children "age up" as they grow stronger). Legally enslaved in
  Melromarc; revered in Siltvelt. Many can shift into a **Therianthrope** form and,
  with training, a stronger **Beast Transformation**.
- **Hakuko** (White Tiger, e.g. Atla/Fohl) and **Orca** (Sadeena/Shildina) are
  rare, powerful demi-human lineages.
- **Slave crests/curses:** magical brands enforcing obedience/honesty and granting
  the owning hero stat bonuses; central to Naofumi's early party (and the story's
  uncomfortable ethics). Naofumi uses them pragmatically; allies sometimes *choose*
  to be crested for the bonuses or as a show of devotion.
- **Filolials:** giant bird-monsters that love pulling carts; a hero-raised one can
  become a **Queen** (Filo, Fitoria) with a human form.
- **Dragons / Dragon Emperors:** apex magical monsters with cores that can be
  harvested to power weapons (Gaelion; the Demon Dragon Emperor).
